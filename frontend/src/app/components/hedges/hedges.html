DTE > 45 HOLD Theta is slow at this duration. No action — let the hedge ride.
DTE 30-45 PLAN Roll trigger approaching. Start scanning fresh 75-100 DTE candidates so you have a target ready.
DTE ≤ 30 ROLL NOW Below 30 DTE, gamma + theta both accelerate fast. Close current hedge and open a new 75-100 DTE put at 5-8% OTM.
Why 3-month? 60d hedges are too short — you'll be rolling every 30d (high friction). 4-6 months are too long — vega risk grows and capital ties up. 75-100 DTE hits the sweet spot: enough buffer for one roll cycle, not so much that vega becomes a liability.
